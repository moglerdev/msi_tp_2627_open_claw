Subject: Invitation to the conference panel. Dear Colleague Solberg, I write in connection with a meeting held by the Philosophische Fakultät of the Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel. I, Prof. Dr. Viktor Hahn, viktor.hahn@example.com, invite you, Prof. Dr. Armin Solberg, armin.solberg@example.com, to the conference panel. The occasion is the conference panel. It meets on 10 September 2026 at 11:30 in conference room 4, castle, Schlossplatz 1, Kiel. I ask for a reply by 27 August 2026. The panel bears the title causality without an experiment. I do not ask for a lesson on causal identification, nor for a formalism supplied after the fact that the title does not contain. Contributions are expected to keep design and claim apart, and objections are expected to address the reasons actually offered. I ask you to shape your contribution so that the title is taken seriously, without letting the discussion become a general lecture on method. No joint declaration of the panel is intended. The faculty expects an exact, bounded and courteous discussion under the title named above, in which each claim remains with what the contribution in question actually shows. Please keep your contribution to a length that leaves room for the other speakers, and mark clearly where you report a finding and where you add an interpretation. The faculty expects disputed passages to be discussed in the wording of the paper rather than replaced by a free-standing remark on method. A written text is welcome, but it is not made a condition.
