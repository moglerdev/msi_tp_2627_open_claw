Subject: Invitation to the thesis defence. Dear Colleague Eller, I write from the Philosophische Fakultät of the Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel. I, Prof. Dr. Armin Solberg, armin.solberg@example.com, invite you, Prof. Dr. Nadja Eller, nadja.eller@example.com, to the thesis defence. The occasion is the thesis defence. It takes place on 18 June 2026 at 10:00 in lecture hall B, Leibnizstraße 8, Kiel. I ask for a reply by 11 June 2026. The candidate is Dr. des. Miriam Falk. The dissertation treats concept drift in longitudinal panels. I do not ask for a subsequent definition of that expression, nor for a general lesson in its use. What should be tested is whether the argument holds together, whether the panels are described with precision, and whether objections are answered on their merits. The discussion should remain with the submitted dissertation and should not turn into a survey of neighbouring procedures. I ask you to read the text beforehand and, in the hall, to put a question that ties one claim of the work to the account the work itself gives. This invitation carries no advance decision on the assessment. The faculty expects an exact, courteous examination confined to the dissertation, in which the candidate presents her reasons herself and the members of the committee follow up only where a claim is left unsupported. For this preparation the submitted dissertation is enough. Please bring no materials the candidate has not seen, and let questions follow the chapter order wherever the exposition allows that sequence.
