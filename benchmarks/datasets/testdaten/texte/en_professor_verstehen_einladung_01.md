Subject: Invitation to the faculty colloquium. Dear Colleague Solberg, I write from the Philosophische Fakultät of the Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel. I, Prof. Dr. Helene Kranz, helene.kranz@example.com, invite you, Prof. Dr. Armin Solberg, armin.solberg@example.com, to the faculty colloquium. The occasion is the faculty colloquium. We meet on 23 April 2026 at 18:15 in the senate hall, Leibnizstraße 8, Kiel. I ask for a reply by 16 April 2026. The paper under discussion concerns the methodological limits of self-report scales. I do not ask you to fix a numerical summary afterwards, nor to supply a ceremonial account of a procedure. What should be examined is how far answers on predetermined scales can support claims about persons when memory, social desirability and the narrowness of the categories shape what is reported. Please look at whether the text states the reach of its conclusions or quietly extends them once the scale has been introduced. I ask you to read the paper beforehand and to note two objections, one to the construction of the questions and one to the interpretation of the answers. No commitment to a particular mode of analysis is intended. The discussion, kept small, should separate what the argument as presented supports from what it merely suggests. If you can sharpen the question in a brief opening remark without anticipating the result, please say so with your reply. The faculty expects a restrained and exact discussion, not a recommendation of a single instrument and not an extension of the paper.
