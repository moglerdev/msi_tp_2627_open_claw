Subject: Invitation to the habilitation lecture. Dear Colleague Holm, I write from the Philosophische Fakultät of the Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel. I, Prof. Dr. Viktor Hahn, viktor.hahn@example.com, invite you, Prof. Dr. Edgar Holm, to the habilitation lecture. The occasion is the habilitation lecture. It takes place on 9 July 2026 at 16:30 in lecture hall B, Leibnizstraße 8, Kiel. I ask for a reply by 2 July 2026. Dr. Paula Renner will speak on model misspecification. I do not ask for a subsequent definition of that expression, nor for a general lesson in its use. The lecture should present the speaker's own argument, and the discussion that follows should remain with the reasons offered. I ask you to take note of the announced line of thought beforehand and, in the hall, to put a question that ties one claim to the account the speaker gives. This invitation carries no advance decision on the procedure. The faculty expects a clearly bounded lecture and an exact, courteous inquiry, which does not turn the topic into a general lesson on models and does not anticipate the judgement. Please frame your question so that the speaker can answer it from the lecture. The faculty expects the discussion to respect the order of the lecture and not to jump into neighbouring topics. A question is welcome when it takes up a claim of the speaker and does not open a paper. Keep the question short enough for other members of the faculty to speak.
