Subject: Invitation to the editorial board meeting. Dear Colleague Hahn, I write from the Philosophische Fakultät of the Christian-Albrechts-Universität zu Kiel. I, Prof. Dr. Helene Kranz, helene.kranz@example.com, invite you, Prof. Dr. Viktor Hahn, viktor.hahn@example.com, to the editorial board meeting. The occasion is the editorial board meeting. We meet on 2 October 2026 at 09:30 in the meeting room, Leibnizstraße 8, Kiel. I ask for a reply by 25 September 2026. The agenda is a special issue on measurement. I do not ask for a final acceptance of individual manuscripts, nor for a subsequent account of what a measurement must accomplish. What should be discussed is which submissions fit the issue, whether the claim about measurement is stated clearly, and which texts are to be placed on the list for further reading. I ask you to read the abstracts received beforehand and to name two submissions whose inclusion you would defend or defer. No decision on the final contents of the issue is intended at this sitting. The faculty expects an exact, bounded and collegial consultation, which settles the shape of the issue and does not anticipate the separate reading of the manuscripts. Please distinguish in your preliminary note between submissions that can carry the issue and submissions that would need another issue. The faculty expects that distinction to be tied to the abstracts, not to a general judgement of taste. No ranking of submissions is to be drawn up. Name only what you can defend in the sitting.
